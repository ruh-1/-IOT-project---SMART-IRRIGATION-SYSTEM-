# 🌱 Smart Irrigation System

An IoT-based smart irrigation system that automatically monitors soil moisture and controls water supply based on the moisture level of the soil.

## 📌 About the Project

The **Smart Irrigation System** is designed to reduce water wastage and automate plant watering. A **soil moisture sensor** continuously checks the moisture level of the soil. When the soil becomes dry, the system automatically turns **ON** the water pump. Once the soil reaches the required moisture level, the pump turns **OFF**.

## ⚙️ How It Works

```text
        🌱 Soil
          ↓
  Soil Moisture Sensor
          ↓
     Microcontroller
          ↓
     Moisture Check
       ↙       ↘
    Dry         Wet
     ↓           ↓
  Pump ON      Pump OFF
     ↓
   💧 Water
```
